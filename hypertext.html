Draw a Rose

Draw a Rose is an interactive project for ART 74 that turns a simple drawing into a journey. Instead of viewing the entire picture at once, visitors move through pages and watch a flower take shape.

Hypertext / Interactive web2026HTML · illustration · video

Enter the rose website ↗

How it works

I used linked HTML pages, illustrations, and a video to break the process of drawing a rose into different steps. Each click advances the story through the stem, leaves, and petals.

Why this format

A hyperlink makes the viewer a participant. The order of the pages becomes part of the artwork, showing how a website can be more than a collection of images.

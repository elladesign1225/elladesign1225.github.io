03 / Classic

Category: Art 74 / Time-Based Mashup

Medium: Video, found game footage, image sequences, FFmpeg, Google Colab, Adobe Premiere

Watch the mashup

Open the Art 74 mashup video directly

Video file: datacenter_resized.mp4

Classic combines two video game worlds: a conversation scene involving Leon in Resident Evil and the Haven map from Valorant. I chose these sources because I enjoy both games and wanted to create a different experience by showing them together.

Process

I worked with Google Colab and FFmpeg to crop and generate frame sequences, then used Premiere to shape the timing and audio. The process involved testing commands, finding file-path errors, and adjusting the crops.

What the mashup changes

Placing two recognizable games in one sequence creates a new way to introduce them to viewers. The combination brings a quiet, story-focused scene together with a competitive game environment.

04 / Draw a Rose: An HTML Hypertext

Category: Art 74 / Hypertext

Medium: HTML, illustration, video, linked pages

Draw a Rose turns a drawing process into a small journey. Each hyperlink moves the visitor to another part of the rose: its stem, petals, and leaves. Once the flower is complete, visitors choose whether to keep it as it is or see a different version.

The order of the pages is part of the story. Rather than viewing all the images at once, visitors build the rose by choosing to keep moving forward.

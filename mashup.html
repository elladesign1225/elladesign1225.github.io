ART 74 / New Media

Classic

Classic is a time-based mashup for ART 74, combining a scene from Resident Evil 9 Requiem with the environment of Valorant’s Haven map. I chose both games because I enjoy their very different storytelling styles.

Time-Based Mashup2026Video · FFmpeg · Premiere

Short video export from the portfolio files. The complete Classic mashup needs to be added before final submission.

Making the mashup

I worked with Google Colab and FFmpeg to prepare frames and crop the different sources to fit together. I used Adobe Premiere to adjust the sequence and audio. Finding the correct file paths, processing images, and troubleshooting errors became an important part of learning.

New meaning

Bringing a quiet cinematic game scene together with the space of a competitive game changes how each source is experienced. The work introduces familiar games through an unexpected visual combination.

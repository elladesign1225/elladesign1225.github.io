Posters, publications, illustrations, and type experiments. These projects explore how hierarchy, color, and form can turn an idea into a clear visual experience.

Editorial / Typography / 2026

Eurostile: Typeface Feature

A two-sided editorial feature exploring Eurostile, a geometric typeface often associated with futuristic design. I balanced letterform examples, imagery, and structured typography to show how the typeface communicates its personality. Developed as a graphic design class project.

Informational Poster / 2026

The History of Type

An informational poster introducing key developments in typography. For this class exercise, I organized historical material with strong contrast, a large typographic title, and a clear reading sequence.

Editorial Design / 2025

Martina Flor Magazine Spread

A magazine-style editorial spread about lettering artist Martina Flor. I experimented with spacing, alignment, and visual hierarchy to make information feel approachable and visually expressive.

Publication Design / 2025

Half Moon Bay Brochure

A destination brochure designed for a graphic design class. I used coastal photography, curves, and a calm color palette to guide viewers through travel information while suggesting the atmosphere of Half Moon Bay.

Book Cover Design / 2025

The Road — Book Cover

A concept cover for Cormac McCarthy’s The Road. Minimal forms and a muted palette translate the themes of travel, uncertainty, and the relationship between a father and son into a single image.

Music Packaging / 2025

YOASOBI Record Cover

A conceptual album cover inspired by my interest in music. Repeated geometry and restrained color help reimagine the record’s atmosphere while giving it a distinct visual identity.

Poster / Typography / 2026

Design Manifesto

A typography-led poster exploring the idea that design carries messages and creates connections between people. Made for a visual communication class, it uses scale and curved forms to emphasize a short statement.

Event Poster / 2025

Electronic Music Festival

An electronic music event poster combining bold typography, gradient colors, and illustrated music equipment. My goal was to create a sense of motion and sound in a static composition.

Event Poster / 2025

CSM Student Voice Concert

A poster designed for a student voice concert featuring Mariachi and Zarzuela. I used warm colors, playful illustrations, and clear event typography to create a welcoming mood for the performance.

Illustration / Poster / 2025

Freedom

A high-contrast illustration built from energetic shapes and expressive marks. This personal visual exploration uses repetition and movement to communicate a sense of freedom.

Digital Illustration / 2025

Suffer Series

Suffer is a series of digital illustrations using geometric shapes and strong color contrasts to reflect discrimination and harm experienced online and in everyday life. Red suggests harm, blue reflects a sense of despair, and green offers hope. The work explores how personal feelings can become visual language.

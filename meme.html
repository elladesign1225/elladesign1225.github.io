ART 74 / New Media

Midnight Terror

Midnight Terror combines SpongeBob, Jerry, and the yellow M&M into one unexpected story. Characters that once belonged to different worlds now share a single scene.

2D Meme Mashup2026Photoshop · digital collage

Process

For this ART 74 mashup assignment, I gathered recognizable memes, used Photoshop layer masks to cut out each character, and arranged them into a scene with a new relationship between the figures.

New meaning

The combination changes their original expressions into a shared moment of surprise. The humor comes from making familiar characters appear unfamiliar together.

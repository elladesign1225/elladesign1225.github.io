About the game

Shy Four is my version of Connect 4. It uses a shy floating flower from the original cursor-chase idea, but the main game is about strategy and taking turns.

Mechanics: Drop a camellia coin in a column. Connect four horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.

Dynamics: Players plan moves, block each other, and respond to the changing board.

Aesthetics: I chose a calm, simple look with cream, forest green, coral, and my white camellia drawing.

About me

I'm Ella Yu, a graphic design student interested in visual storytelling, typography, digital media, and the ways people interact with images.

Artist statement

I am Ella Yu, a graphic design student who enjoys exploring how images, words, and technology can connect people. I see design as a way to share information, tell stories, and help viewers understand an idea. I want my work to communicate clearly, even when it uses very few words.

My interests include typography, geometric shapes, editorial design, digital images, and motion. I like to experiment with color, contrast, and visual hierarchy to guide the eye. I work with Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects, and Premiere, and I am also learning how simple HTML links can change the way people experience a story.

As a new media artist, I enjoy taking familiar material and giving it a different meaning. In a meme mashup, I combine recognizable characters to create a new moment. In glitch work, I explore the unexpected beauty of digital errors. In my rose hypertext project, visitors build a drawing by moving from one page to another and choosing their own direction.

I believe good design should not only be seen, but also make people feel understood and remembered. I am still finding my style. For me, each project is a chance to learn, solve a problem, and try a different way of connecting with someone.

What I work with

  • Print and publication design: posters, brochures, and editorial layouts
  • Typography, grids, color, and composition
  • Digital collage, glitch experiments, and HTML hypertext
  • Motion graphics and video editing

01 / Glitch: Breaking the Image

Category: Art 74 / New Media

Medium: Digital image manipulation

In this experiment, I explored how errors and interruptions can change an image. Digital artifacts, shifts, and unexpected distortions become part of the artwork instead of something to erase.

I am interested in how the same material can look different when its normal structure is disrupted.

ART 74 / New Media

Glitch: Breaking the Image

What happens when a digital image stops behaving as expected? In ART 74, I explored the visual possibilities of unexpected errors and interruption.

Glitch / Digital image manipulation2026Digital image

What I made

I experimented with how an image changes when its normal structure is disrupted. Blocks of color, strange textures, and image artifacts turn a recognizable image into something more abstract.

Why it matters

Glitch art challenges the idea that everything digital must be clean and perfect. It made me consider mistakes as a starting point for visual exploration.

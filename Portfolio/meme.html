02 / Midnight Terror

Category: Art 74 / 2D Meme Mashup

Medium: Digital collage, Photoshop

Midnight Terror combines SpongeBob, Jerry, and the yellow M&M into a playful, slightly scary situation. The happy atmosphere becomes unsettling when the M&M suddenly appears, catching the characters off guard.

Process

I looked for recognizable character images, brought them into Photoshop, used layer masks to separate them from their backgrounds, and arranged the images into a scene with a new story.

New meaning

By bringing familiar memes together, I wanted to turn their original reactions into one new moment of surprise. The humor comes from seeing characters from different worlds share the same strange event.

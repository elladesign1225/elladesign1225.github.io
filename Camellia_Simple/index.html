Camellia — Shape Sketchbook

Press 1–0 to switch between ten simple shape brushes. Click or drag to draw.

Selected brush: 1 — Line

S = save   X = clear   Z = undo

Ten brushes

  • 1 Line — thin straight stroke
  • 2 Circle — solid round marks
  • 3 Square — solid blocks
  • 4 Triangle — pointed shapes
  • 5 Diamond — rotated squares
  • 6 Ring — empty circles
  • 7 Star — five-point shapes
  • 8 Dash — small marks
  • 9 Plus — crosses
  • 0 Dots — clusters of tiny circles

This drawing tool focuses on basic geometry, repetition, and pattern. The limited colors and simple marks make abstract compositions easy, but realistic textures and detailed portraits difficult.

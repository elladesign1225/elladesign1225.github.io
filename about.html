Meet Ella

Graphic designer & new media student

I'm Ella Yu, a graphic design student at San José State University. I'm interested in visual communication and the way people experience information through images, type, movement, and interaction. I enjoy learning different techniques and connecting them to my own ideas.

Artist statement

Design is a way to make ideas visible. I am a learner, thinker, and designer who believes that visual work can communicate a message without needing many words. I like using color, type, shapes, and composition to guide attention and help people connect with an idea.

Before I begin a project, I like to research, collect references, and sketch different approaches. I then experiment using tools such as Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, Premiere, and After Effects. My work moves between printed posters, editorial layouts, digital collage, and moving images. I enjoy discovering how different tools change the way a story can be told.

In ART 74, I have been exploring the possibilities of new media. Glitch images reveal unexpected digital effects. Meme mashups change the meaning of familiar characters. Video editing creates connections across time, and hypertext allows viewers to choose their own way through a story. These experiments help me think beyond a single image or screen.

I want my work to feel thoughtful, clear, and memorable. Good design should not only be seen; it should help people feel understood. I am still developing my visual style, and every project gives me a new opportunity to explore, improve, and grow.

Welcome to my portfolio

Hello! I’m Ella, a graphic design student exploring typography, composition, storytelling, and new media. I enjoy combining clear structure with playful experiments to communicate ideas through images, words, and movement.

This portfolio is a place to move between different ways of making: from printed posters and editorial design to glitch images, videos, and a rose that grows through hyperlinks.

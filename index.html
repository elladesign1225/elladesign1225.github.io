About the artist

Design should do more than look good.

I'm interested in how color, composition, typography, and technology can create meaning. Whether I am experimenting with images in ART 74 or building an editorial layout, I want viewers to feel connected to the idea behind the work.

My approach mixes research, sketching, and digital experimentation — and I'm still growing with each project.

Typeface

Eurostile is one of those fonts that instantly feels futuristic, even decades after it was created. Originally developed from the earlier typeface Microgramma in the 1950s, Eurostile was refined in 1962 by Aldo Novarese, who expanded the design to include lowercase letters.

This change helped transform it from a rigid, all-caps system into a more versatile and widely usable typeface. Its geometric shapes also helped Eurostile become popular in modern and futuristic design.

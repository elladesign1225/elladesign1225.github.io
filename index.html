About the game

Shy Four is my version of Connect 4. I started with the class's ball-chase game and kept that idea in the floating flower. I changed the main game into something about strategy and taking turns.

Mechanics: Drop a flower into a column and connect four horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Dynamics: Players plan their next moves and block each other.

Aesthetics: I wanted a calm, simple style using my white camellia illustration and just three main colors.
